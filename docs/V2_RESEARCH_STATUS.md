# V2 foundations and execution-research status

The v2 foundation is split across sibling repositories:

```text
blox/       deterministic matching engine and TCP transport
algosim/    executable multi-market simulator and market-data service
algolib/    canonical orders, market model and provider adapters
algorunner/ deterministic parent/child lifecycle orchestration
algovisual/ original blox-visualize terminal, packaged for reuse
algotest/   integrated visual laboratory and research/TCA runner
```

## Completed integration

- `algovisual` preserves the historical `blox-visualize` portal, chart, book,
  panels and `OrderEntry` instead of introducing a separate laboratory UI.
- `OrderEntry` receives the canonical 22-family catalog from `algolib/orders`.
- the market selector switches among all simulator profiles;
- visual updates carry Level 2 depth rather than only best bid/ask;
- orders pass through `algorunner` and execute in the same `blox-core` book
  that produces market data;
- passive fills generated by background agents return to the parent lifecycle;
- the laboratory listens on `0.0.0.0:8090` when launched by the current runbook.

## Simulation and research

The simulator now provides multiple market makers per level, irregular shaped
depth, persistent aggressive flow, momentum, clustered volatility, queue
imbalance, dynamic spread, deterministic seeds and explicit price, volatility,
volume and liquidity events. Ten research profiles cover liquid, sparse,
trending, gapped, bearish, high-volatility, deep-volatility, thin-fast,
ultra-liquid and stressed markets.

The full synthetic TCA executes 145,200 isolated parent orders and stores
763,799 raw fills. It spans ten seeds, ten markets, eleven scenarios, three
depth-relative sizes, 22 families and both sides. It includes opening/closing
auctions, imbalance phase, price spikes/dips, volume bursts, volatility shock,
liquidity crisis and combined stress.

## Model-risk position

The output is a serious deterministic synthetic TCA, not live-grade venue TCA.
Before production use it requires Level 2/3 calibration, independent market
VWAP/TWAP and auction benchmarks, opportunity cost, markouts, fees/rebates,
venue attribution, latency and monetary tick-value models. Isolated causal
experiments and any future concurrent-cohort experiment must remain separate.
